# Campaign background generation

Mode: OpenAI built-in image-generation workflow.

Reference images: repository files `zaviv-05.png` and `zaviv-06.png`, used only as visual-language references.

Final source: `campaign-background.png`.

## Prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: reusable vertical campaign background plate for Zaviv social posts and video
Primary request: create a premium cinematic technology background that matches the visual language of the reference assets without copying their text or phone UI
Scene/backdrop: deep matte black space with a subtle curved digital grid floor, sparse warm orange particles glowing from the upper left, sparse electric blue particles glowing from the lower right, very faint central halo for a product silhouette
Style/medium: polished cinematic 3D technology advertising background, refined and restrained
Composition/framing: portrait 4:5, symmetrical center, generous clean negative space in the top third and center for later product and typography compositing, strongest detail only around outer edges
Lighting/mood: dark, premium, confident, precise
Color palette: near-black, charcoal, ember orange, electric blue, small white highlights
Constraints: background plate only; no device, no phone, no app interface, no logo, no words, no letters, no icons, no watermark; preserve a clean uncluttered center; social-ad polish
Avoid: generic neon tunnel, cyberpunk city, purple, green, excessive lens flare, busy circuitry, illegible fake UI, text
```
